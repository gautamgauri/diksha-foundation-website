01 · Why we exist

Every child deserves a place where they are seen, heard and invited to discover who they can become.

Education cannot be reduced to literacy, marks or employability. At Diksha, it is a journey of self-discovery—and a way to build more thoughtful relationships with oneself, the community and the world. Our three Rs— Relationships, Responsibility and Reverence for all life—hold this work together.

Our story

A promise made in Bihar.

In 1997, a group of school friends in Patna volunteered for community service and began imagining a fairer future for children in Bihar.

Thirteen years later, they came together again to establish Diksha Foundation. Since 2010, that early promise has grown into long-term learning relationships with children, young people, families and schools.

We remain rooted in Bihar while continuing to learn from educators, communities and partners across India and the world.

In loving memory of Badi Maa (1956–2019) A teacher and guide whose kindness, simplicity and compassion continue to be reflected in Diksha’s work.
