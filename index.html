01 · Why Diksha exists

Every child deserves an education that recognises their full humanity.

Academic learning matters. So do confidence, relationships, imagination, health, creativity and the ability to participate in the world.

Diksha stays with children and communities over time. We create spaces where a child can be known, supported and challenged—not reduced to a score, a deficit or a single definition of success.

  • Joy
  • Democracy

Evidence and learning

We look for progress we can name—and questions that help us improve.

Attendance, learning assessments, digital practice, meals and children’s own participation help educators understand what is changing and where more support is needed.

A programme-level example

Regularity and learning moved together at Sarairanjan.

Programme records reported that 27 children who had been attending school irregularly became regular, while the centre’s average score moved from 50% to 65%.

Sarairanjan programme records, April 2025. Meal and digital-learning figures are from KHEL programme records for 2025–26.

Long-term presence

Change is held by relationships.

Diksha does not enter a community for a short project and disappear.

Children grow through sustained relationships with educators, families, peers and the wider community. Over time, former learners return as educators and mentors—carrying the work forward in their own way.

Our story

A promise made in Bihar.

In 1997, a group of school friends in Patna volunteered for community service and began imagining a fairer future for children in Bihar.

Thirteen years later, they came together again to establish Diksha Foundation. Since 2010, that early promise has grown into lasting learning relationships with children, young people, families and schools.

We remain rooted in Bihar while learning with educators, communities and partners across India and the world.

In loving memory of Badi Maa (1956–2019) A teacher and guide whose kindness, simplicity and compassion continue to be reflected in Diksha’s work.
