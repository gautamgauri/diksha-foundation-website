Change we can see and learn from

A child’s expanding agency is the outcome that holds the others together.

We look for academic progress—and for voice, belonging, participation, creative confidence and the ability to navigate opportunity.

01 · What changes

Learning becomes visible in what children are increasingly able to do.

↗

Learn with confidence

Build academic foundations, ask for help and persist through difficulty.

↗

Speak and participate

Express an idea, ask a question and take part in collective decisions.

↗

Create and solve

Use art, science and technology to imagine, make and respond to real problems.

↗

Navigate possibility

Recognise aspirations, access opportunities and make informed choices about the future.

02 · A current example

Sarairanjan: learning, return and participation

Recent programme reporting from Sarairanjan shows why attendance, learning and community trust must be read together.

Learning

50% → 65%

Average assessment scores increased by 15 percentage points in the reported period.

Regularity

27 learners

Children previously attending irregularly began participating regularly.

School continuity

Girls returned

Learners who had disengaged or dropped out were supported to re-enter education and continue school transitions.

Community

Home + school

Parent engagement, home visits and collaboration with government teachers strengthened the circle around each child.

“Now I talk to everyone and I’m not afraid. The centre showed me that we are all equal.”
Shivani, Sarairanjan KHEL learner

03 · Longer journeys

From learner to leader

The most powerful evidence often appears across years: when a young person carries confidence, care and agency into the next stage of life.

Alumni pathway

Priyanka

A former KHEL learner who went on to join Bihar Police—and became a visible possibility for younger children in her community.

Alumni pathway

Maya

She joined Diksha as a learner, later became a KHEL educator, and continued her postgraduate education in sociology.

These stories are not presented as proof by themselves. They sit alongside attendance, assessment, school progression, observation and programme reporting so that Diksha can learn honestly from both progress and gaps.
