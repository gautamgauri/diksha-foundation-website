Impact

We look at what children can do now that they could not do before.

Marks in Hindi and mathematics matter to us, as does a child’s readiness to ask a question in Bal Sansad or to explain her work to a visitor.

01 · What changes

Four kinds of change we look for.

↗

Learn with confidence

Build academic foundations, ask for help and persist through difficulty.

↗

Speak and participate

Express an idea, ask a question and take part in collective decisions.

↗

Create and solve

Use art, science and technology to imagine, make and respond to real problems.

↗

Choose what comes next

Know which options are open after school and make an informed choice between them.

02 · A current example

KHEL Sarairanjan in 2025–26

The centre’s report for April 2025 to March 2026 sets its learning results beside the attendance figures.

Hindi

35.8% → 76.5%

The average Hindi score of 148 learners, from baseline to endline in FY 2025–26.

Attendance

101 a day

On average, 101 of the 182 enrolled children came each day, about 55 per cent.

Girls

129 of 182

Girls made up 129 of the 182 children enrolled during the year.

Community

Home + school

Teaching Fellows visited families every fortnight and ran regular sessions in four, later five, nearby government schools.

“Now I talk to everyone and I’m not afraid.”
Anjali, a learner at KHEL Sarairanjan (name changed)
