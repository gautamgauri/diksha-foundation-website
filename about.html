A Bihar-rooted journey

A promise between friends became a long relationship with learning.

Diksha was founded in Patna in 2010 to create educational opportunity without reducing education to marks, literacy or employment alone.

01 · Our story

From 1997 to today

In 1997, a group of school friends from Don Bosco Academy, Patna, volunteered for community service and began imagining a fairer educational future in their home state.

Thirteen years later, they came together again to establish Diksha Foundation. The early work began with a simple conviction: children from socially and economically marginalised communities deserve not only access to schooling, but spaces that nurture their full human potential.

Since 2010, Diksha has grown through learning centres, school partnerships, youth programmes, scholarships, civic education and community initiatives. The form has evolved; the central commitment—to relationships and the whole child—has remained.

In loving memory

Badi Maa
1956–2019

A teacher, friend and guide whose kindness and compassion continue to shape Diksha’s moral imagination.

She was remembered for her simplicity, elegance, generosity and ability to make people feel cared for. She taught those around her to find contentment, spread happiness and live with compassion.

Her spirit remains present in the warmth, care and reverence that Diksha tries to bring to every learning relationship.
