English Access

At Access, English is not taught only through grammar books. Students discuss a film, write an email, interview one another, rehearse a play and present work they have made together.

Our first Access group brought together 50 young people in Patna. A second group of 50 first-year STEM students began classes at NIT Patna in September 2026. In both programmes, students use English in discussions, presentations and projects.

The first programme · from 2020

Fifty students joined us during Covid.

When classes began in September 2020, everyone met on Zoom. As restrictions eased, the students began meeting in person. They worked in two groups of 25 until the programme closed.

01

English for everyday and professional life

Students read the news together and took part in group discussions. They also wrote formal emails, prepared CVs and practised job interviews.

02

Films and group discussions

A film could lead to a conversation about gender, identity or the media. Students also compared parts of everyday life in India and the United States.

03

Theatre and filmmaking

Students made photographs and films, wrote poetry, performed music and rehearsed plays. English was something they needed in order to finish and share the work.

04

Kolkata and Santiniketan

During the 2022 intensive visit, the group visited places connected with art and history, took part in theatre and team activities, and used English away from the classroom.

By the end of the first programme

The students led their own closing event.

Families watched the students perform a play, recite poetry, share music and screen a film. The students also spoke about what it had been like to begin the programme on Zoom.

50 students

Completed the programme

Their English was assessed when they joined and again when they left.

3 alumni

Returned as KHEL volunteers

They came back each week to share dance, music and craft with younger children at KHEL.

U.S. exchange

Study in the United States

Some Diksha alumni later went to the United States on exchange programmes, including the Community College Initiative Program.

The new phase · NIT Patna

The second Access group studies at NIT Patna.

Fifty first-year STEM students were selected from 111 applicants in September 2026. The programme was inaugurated at NIT Patna on 22 September and classes began on 26 September. It runs into 2028, with 200 hours of learning for each student.

An engineering student has to do more than understand an idea. They also have to explain it, answer questions and work with a team. The NIT Patna classes are planned around those situations.

Learning at NIT Patna

How English will be used at NIT Patna.

Classes began in late September 2026. The activities below come from the programme plan; this page will carry the students’ own projects and reflections as the year goes on.

01

Explain a technical idea clearly

Students will practise explaining scientific and technical ideas in English, then respond to questions from others.

02

Work on a problem as a team

Applied projects, an innovation challenge and a hackathon will give students work to plan and complete together.

03

Present work and respond to feedback

Students will make technical presentations and prepare a final capstone for an audience beyond their own group.

04

Visits and community work

Meetings with educators and professionals, community work and exposure to U.S. academic communication are part of the plan.

The partnership

The partners behind Access in Patna.

The first Access programme was implemented by Diksha Foundation with the U.S. Consulate General Kolkata and RELO India.

In the new phase, NIT Patna hosts the students and Diksha runs the programme, with the Regional English Language Office of the U.S. Embassy in New Delhi.
