01 · KHEL

Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning

KHEL is Diksha’s flagship after-school programme: a dependable neighbourhood space where learning is joyful, participatory and connected to the whole child.

Children receive support in language, mathematics, science and digital learning, alongside social-emotional learning, arts, sport, civic education, mentoring and opportunities to lead. Open houses, Bal Sansad, parent meetings and community visits make families part of the learning relationship.
