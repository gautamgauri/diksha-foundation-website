03 · Principles

Governance that protects people and purpose

Diksha is a non-profit organisation that is independent of political parties and religious institutions.

We expect collaborators to have documented safeguarding practices for children, young people and staff. Organisational decisions should protect dignity, privacy, fairness and the responsible use of resources.
